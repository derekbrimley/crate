Established by the five foundation documents. Every number below is owned by the document named; link to it instead of restating it, and never contradict it.

**From [the data model](foundations/data-model.md).** Four things are stored: the account, the records, the picks, the settings. Everything else the user sees is derived on the spot and stored nowhere — crate contents, play counts, last-played dates, release years, genre badges, already-in-library, duplicate groups, coverage gaps. Metadata has three fields in practice (`genres`, `release_date`, `total_tracks`), is best-effort, and nothing ever fills in genres. Removing a record removes its picks. Re-filing an album already in the library overwrites it, keeping its id and its picks but resetting its filed-at time, its list, and its metadata. Uniqueness is by Spotify id, so deluxe and remastered editions are separate records. Everything durable is whole seconds since the epoch; playback alone is milliseconds. Crate definitions are one setting, so any save rewrites all of them. A pick stores the crate's machine-generated id, not its name.

**From [the account and the session](foundations/account-and-session.md).** Two independent states: **signed in** (enough for the crate wall, the library, the editor, the log, filter rules, the album search, and an album's tracks and related albums — everything the server fetches with Crate's own Spotify credentials) and **linked to Spotify** (required for the import tabs and playback — everything that acts as the user on Spotify). An email account can never be linked. There is no account screen and no "connected" indicator; the only place the distinction is named is the two import tabs' connect prompt. The first session check is the only full-page loading state and it treats "cannot ask" as "not signed in". The account row is re-synced on every arrival. Spotify tokens are refreshed server-side, within a minute of expiry or once on rejection, invisibly; the stored expiry is assumed to be an hour, not read from Spotify. Sign-out is global, across tabs and devices, with no confirmation. Thirteen crate definitions are seeded on a new account's first crate-wall load.

**From [navigation and loading](foundations/navigation-and-loading.md).** Five routes: `/`, `/library`, `/add`, `/history`, `/callback` (which is the crate wall). Two bottom-nav items: CRATES and LIBRARY. No back button anywhere and no not-found route. The **session cache** loads each screen once per session: the crate wall loads the wall *and* the whole library *and* the whole log; `/library` loads only the library; `/history` only the log; `/add` nothing. Loaded means the fetch *succeeded*: a failed load leaves the screen not loaded, so nothing retries while the user stays on it and everything retries the next time it is opened. A crate wall whose load failed shows no rows at all, because the crate definitions arrive with the same fetch. Five things update the cache: a crate's refresh button, saving a definition, reordering or deleting a crate, a slow crate finishing, and the library's optimistic local edits. Nothing polls, expires, or refetches on focus. Skeletons appear on first load only. The album detail panel keeps a separate never-invalidated cache keyed by Spotify id.

**From [the selection engine](foundations/selection-engine.md).** Pool first (filter rules), then draw (the strategy). Six filter fields with fixed operators; a blank-value rule is ignored; a record with no release date fails every year rule; a record with no genres satisfies "genre is not X"; "artist is" matches the whole comma-joined credit line. Default weighting: cooldown 3 days → weight 0; under 14 days → 1; under 30 days → 3; beyond that or never → 5; never-picked bonus +2; recently-added bonus +0 within 14 days, and the seeded crates force it off. The weight is multiplied by a random number raised to 1 ÷ the variety setting (default 1.0) and the top few are drawn without replacement. The editor exposes four of the ten numbers as five-stop sliders (cooldown 0/3/7/14/31; variety 2.0/1.5/1.0/0.7/0.4; discovery 0/1/2/3/5; recently added 0/1/2/3/5); the other six cannot be changed anywhere. Five strategies: weighted, random, pool with AI, new with AI, hybrid (one third AI, rounded, at least one). Cooldown and weights apply to weighted and hybrid only. Count is 1–7; a hand-made crate starts at 4, a seeded crate at 2.

**From [playback](foundations/playback.md).** Four routes tried in a fixed order and silent at every step: hand off by navigating to Spotify (phone and tablet only, and the only route tried there); the in-tab web player (desktop, Premium, linked, device within five seconds); ask Spotify to play on the active device; open Spotify's website in a new tab. "No active Spotify device found" is never shown to the user. The player bar appears only when Crate's own in-tab player is playing, so a successful play elsewhere is indistinguishable from a failure. Playing writes nothing — only picking does. A reload is the one navigation that stops the music. Every screen's bottom padding grows when the bar is showing.

Established by the crates documents as they land.

**From [the crate wall](crates/the-crate-wall.md).** The wall is a sticky green CRATES header — a new-crate button and a profile menu whose only two items are View History and Sign Out — over one shelf per crate. Every control commits at once; there is no dirty state on the wall. A shelf's spines divide the row's width between them, so the crate's count decides their shape and the switch between a rotated vertical title and a horizontal title-plus-artist is at 90 px. The refresh arrow spins for a fixed 600 ms regardless of the request. Reordering swaps neighbours, renumbers, and writes all the definitions without re-running anything. The ★ on a spine appears on hover only, is unreachable on touch, fills in from the tap rather than the answer, and re-runs the whole crate. Which record is selected is one value for the whole screen. There is no empty state for the wall itself, and no way to reach the add screen from it.

**From [picking a record](crates/picking-a-record.md).** Selecting is not picking: tapping a spine writes nothing, and only PLAY ON SPOTIFY or a track tap does. Each writes exactly one pick — the record, the moment, and the crate's id — fired before the playback chain and not awaited, so a pick is recorded whether or not anything plays, and every track tap is another pick. Suggestions record nothing. The panel's LAST PLAYED and PLAYS come from the session cache and never update after a pick; both read `—` when there are no picks. The panel is a panel on the wall (no backdrop, no Escape) and a modal on the listening log. Picks cannot be deleted except by removing the record.

**From [the crate editor](crates/the-crate-editor.md).** The only settings surface in the product, and the only real dirty state: everything in it is local until SAVE, and CANCEL, the ✕, and the dimmed page all discard it silently. Escape does nothing. It covers the bottom navigation and the player bar, so tapping either closes the modal instead. Editable: name (blank → "Untitled Crate"), count (seven buttons, 1–7), filter rules, strategy, the four sliders, the prompt. Not editable and with no control at all: source and position. FILTERS starts expanded here; MATCH AND/OR appears only with two or more rules. There is no preview of what the crate would contain. SAVE writes all the definitions, re-runs that one crate, then closes; a failed save leaves the modal open with the edit intact and says nothing. DELETE CRATE arms and stays armed, and is offered only for a crate that already has a name. Opening a HYBRID crate loads the default sliders rather than its own, so saving it resets them — a bug.

**From [AI crates](crates/ai-crates.md).** Three strategies ask Claude; a new account is seeded with exactly one of them, SURPRISE ME (AI · NEW, no vibe). AI · NEW and HYBRID are always slow crates, and AI · LIBRARY is one only when its vibe is not blank — an AI · LIBRARY crate with a blank vibe is a weighted draw and Claude is never asked. The server leaves slow crates out of the wall's first answer because running them would add about five seconds; the client then fetches each one exactly once, all at once, and never retries, so a failed AI shelf stays empty for the life of the page. A **suggestion** carries the ◈ recommendation badge plus a 2 px cyan bar; it cannot be starred, removed, or promoted, playing it records no pick, and it is discarded on every reload — nothing anywhere records what was suggested. The panel offers ★ FAV and ◈ REC instead, which file a real record; the spine on the shelf goes on being a suggestion until that crate is refreshed. Claude is asked for exactly five albums whatever the crate's count. Every failure falls back silently: AI · LIBRARY to a weighted draw with the engine's default numbers rather than the crate's sliders, AI · NEW to a random draw from the whole library ignoring the crate's rules, HYBRID to more weighted picks. An account with no favorites gets no suggestions at all. AI crates work fully without a Spotify link.
